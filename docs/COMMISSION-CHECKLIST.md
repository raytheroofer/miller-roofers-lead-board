## Clock

All times are Jacksonville. This clock sits on top of the six steps. It does not rename them.

- Wednesday 4 p.m.: the office turns the packet in. Status stays **Review**. The 9 a.m. cutoff is retired.
- Tuesday 4 p.m.: Raymond signs the exact version that was in by the prior Wednesday.
- Friday at noon: the authorized payer pays only that approved version.
- A packet that misses Wednesday stays in Review until the next Tuesday.

Customer-review bonuses are not commission packets.

## The six steps

### 1. Prepare the job — status: Review

- [ ] Open that job’s sheet and Closeout Checklist.
- [ ] Match Roofr job ID, customer, address, and rep. Keep one current cap-out link.
- [ ] Reconcile revenue and customer receipts. Count lender fees once.
- [ ] Price labor from [LABOR COSTS](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1DUFFekwpX_sprq8igBU4VUO6SfFM50Ao5izM7YU6GEg/edit): quantity times the sheet rate. If the sheet says NA, price that item per job and leave the standard rate blank.
- [ ] Leave an unknown cost blank. Do not turn it into zero.
- [ ] List each open item with an owner, the evidence needed, and the resolution.
- [ ] Check a row only when it is done, with an evidence link and initials and date.

### 2. Independent check — hold until resolved

- [ ] The reviewer recomputes costs, profit, commission rate, paid draws, and the proposed balance.
- [ ] Match each cross-job adjustment to both job IDs, the amount, the owner authorization, and one transfer reference.
- [ ] Confirm the same amount has not already been paid.
- [ ] A negative result does not deduct from another job unless Raymond approves that loss-share basis, amount, and destination.
- [ ] A zero or negative payable gets Raymond’s disposition. It does not trigger a cash payment.

### 3. Owner sign-off — Tuesday 4 p.m.

- [ ] Enter Approved amount, Approved by, and Approval date as a fixed amount, a name, and a date. The approved amount is not a formula.
- [ ] On an offset, Approved loss-share amount is the authorized offset, not a new payment.
- [ ] Any later change to inputs, rate, payee, or calculation returns the job to Review and needs a new approval. Keep the earlier approval.

### 4. File the approved packet

- [ ] Save a dated PDF of the populated cap-out and the completed checklist only. Do not export the whole workbook.
- [ ] Name it `JobID — Customer — Cap-Out — YYYY-MM-DD — v1 — APPROVED.pdf`.
- [ ] File a positive payable in 02 — Approved for Payment.
- [ ] File an approved offset in 03 — Paid or Closed by Offset as CLOSED BY OFFSET — DESTINATION PAYMENT PENDING until settlement is verified.

### 5A. Positive payable — Friday by noon

- [ ] Confirm the approved version, the fixed amount, and the recipient. Keep bank details out of the shared sheet.
- [ ] Leave Payment release on HOLD until the checks above are done. Raymond releases it.
- [ ] Pay in the normal payment system. A checkbox is not a payment.
- [ ] Record Payment reference, Payment date, Payment amount, and Payment receipt link.
- [ ] Mark Sent — awaiting reconciliation. If a payment fails, keep the reference and do not send a second payment until that one is explained.

### 5B. Offset — no separate payment

- [ ] Record both job IDs, the fixed amount, the version, and the transfer reference once.
- [ ] Reduce the destination commission once. Do not also enter the transfer as a job expense.
- [ ] The source checklist states that there is no separate payment.
- [ ] Debarry stays closed by offset to Rohatgi. Rohatgi’s exceptions stay open until they are resolved. This page does not approve either amount.

### 6. Reconcile, then QuickBooks, then close

- [ ] Match the cleared bank transaction to the fixed amount, payee, and payment reference. Enter it once.
- [ ] Mark Paid — reconciled, or Closed by offset — settled.
- [ ] Push the job to QuickBooks only after all customer money is collected. That entry is the last step before the job is closed as paid in full and finalized.
- [ ] Leave QuickBooks alone while any customer money is still outstanding.
